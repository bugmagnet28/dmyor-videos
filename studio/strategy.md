## Audience (hypothesis)
People 18 to 32 with big goals and no structure: students, new grads, career switchers, self-improvement viewers. They scroll for motivation but save what is useful.

## Content pillars (test all, keep what wins)
1. **Hard truths about goals** (one sharp line, visualized): "You don't lack motivation. You lack a next step."
2. **Useful tools**: 3-question prompts, mini frameworks (plan backwards, the 2-action week), checklists to save.
3. **The year in numbers**: days left in 2026, weeks until January, "what you can still do in 12 weeks".
4. **Show the app**: a real dmyor canvas turning a dream into steps, fast.

## Format defaults (from the diagnosis; experiments may change them)
12 to 25s, frame 0 = big bold statement, change every 1 to 1.5s, burned-in text, viewer-first, brand card last 2 to 3s, a reason to save or share.

## Current bets (first 7 days)
1. Test frame-0 hook + short length on every video (V-EXP-01, V-EXP-02).
2. Rotate pillars 1 to 4 so we learn which topic pulls.
3. Get real numbers in: ask Sanyam for screenshots 48h after each post.
