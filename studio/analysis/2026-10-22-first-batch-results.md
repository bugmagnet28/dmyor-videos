1. **Instagram is showing each reel to a tiny test group and stopping.** Reach 2 to 65, ~100% non-followers. Nothing is being punished (Account Status clean); the reels are not winning the first test batch. Zero shares/comments/follows = no signal that pushes a reel further.
2. **Posting 12 in ~23 hours hurt.** The reels competed for the same small test audience; later slots did worst (Europe 23:30 reached 2). Region cannot be read from this: each slot had a different reel, and the effect of being the 10th post of the day is bigger than the time zone.
3. **What did best is a short, contrarian, visual statement.** "SOMEDAY is not a day" (week list, hook word struck out) reached 65, 2x anything else. Next: "Your plan only works on good days" (29) and "Never miss twice" (24). Worst were instructional/quiz formats: "start at the end" (5), "3 goals, only 1 is a plan" quiz (2), "3 questions" (6). Hypothesis: one sharp truth beats a framework or a quiz.
4. **The format itself is likely the ceiling.** All 12 look alike: flat color + text. Real-footage reels on this same account did 136 to 185. Text-on-color reels are easy to swipe past at frame 0.
5. **No call to action is landing.** 0 comments despite "drop it below" captions.
