# Instagram niche scan: what works in goals, habits and productivity (2026-10-25)

**Method.** Claude browsed Instagram from @becauseidmyor (Browser 2), slowly, to protect the account. It covered:
- the top posts for the searches "goal setting", "procrastination" and "habits";
- each post's likes / comments / shares, hook frame, caption and comments;
- the reels grids of one planner brand and one mid-size creator, where Instagram shows view counts.

That is 18 posts and 40 grid reels. No bulk scraping. Instagram's web search favours older viral posts, and only the grids show views, so treat these as directional signals, not statistics.

## What's working (in order of evidence)

1. **A human or a hand is on screen in every top reel.** That means a talking head, a podcast clip, a person at a whiteboard, or hands writing on paper.
   - The only faceless formats near the top are an archive clip (Brian Tracy) and a one-line quote card from 2023.
   - **We found no polished faceless motion-graphics explainers (our current style) among the top posts.** That's a risk, but it could also be a gap we fill.
2. **Handwriting and paper are the faceless look that works.** Notebooks, whiteboards, planners and pen strokes (#2, #5, #7, #9, #12, the Passion Planner top 3). It reads as "a real person's method", which is what this niche trusts.
3. **Contrarian or hard-truth hooks with a curiosity gap.** "The most dangerous lie", "You won't succeed (until you understand this)", "this video can LITERALLY change your life", "How to: achieve unrealistic goals". Our hard-truth hooks are in the right family; the winners add a gap ("until you understand this").
4. **Shares are the growth metric, and identity lines get shared.** "Doing your thing is doing the thing" (22.7K shares), "You won't succeed…" (10.7K), "The most dangerous lie" (18.9K). These are short lines people send to a friend or post as their story.
5. **Comment-keyword funnels drive comments.** "Comment DOCUMENT / WORKSHOP / GOT IT / detox and I'll send you…". Comment counts jump (#7: 2.7K; #18: 818 comments on 1.3K likes). Comments are a strong ranking signal, and the reply DM moves people off-platform.
6. **Relatable identity beats method lists.** "It has to be perfect so I'll do it later" (perfectionist) and "you're not lazy" style lines outperform "7 steps" lists.
7. **Carousels in this niche are low-reach but saveable.** Yellow-highlight type, "Save this", 3-35K likes. The planner brand uses them to complement reels, not as the growth engine.
8. **Series hooks keep people following.** For example "Ep 0/$40 to $100k". The viewer follows to see the next episode.
9. **Product use done well.** Passion Planner's best reels show the product being used by hands, plus one how-to ("roadmap for your goals"), not announcements. Apps get promoted through POV creators (#11).

## How we compare

- **Our reels:**
  - Best Instagram reel so far: 141 views (tree-study-abroad).
  - YouTube Shorts on Oct 25: 71 and 37 views after a few hours.
  - Followers flat at 21.
- **What we already do right:**
  - hard-truth hooks;
  - one clear method per reel with a logical example;
  - a finished frame 0;
  - consistent look and voice;
  - a value close.
- **What's missing compared with the niche:**
  - **no human or handmade feel** (ours is clean vector motion graphics);
  - **no curiosity gap in the hook**;
  - **no "sendable" one-liner**;
  - **no comment funnel**;
  - **no series**;
  - **nothing showing Dmyor being used**.

## Recommendations (ranked)

1. **Build the Whiteboard / paper-notebook style next**, ahead of Dark Keynote. Handwritten strokes on paper or a whiteboard are the closest faceless match to what wins here. The Lemo-Opuscar `whiteboard` prompt is saved in `reference/lemo-opuscar/styles/whiteboard/`. Then A/B it against the illustrated style on the same hooks.
2. **Rewrite hooks with a curiosity gap.** Keep the hard truth and add "until you understand this", "here's the part nobody tells you", "the most dangerous lie about goals". Test 2 of these per batch.
3. **One sendable line per reel.** One sentence built to be shared or reposted ("Doing your thing is doing the thing" energy). Put it on screen as the land card.
4. **Comment funnel on 1 reel a day.** "Comment PLAN and I'll send you the goal-breakdown template." This needs:
   - a free template (a dmyor.com page or a PDF);
   - someone to reply by DM. Sanyam replies manually at our current volume. Auto-DM tools (ManyChat) only after checking they're allowed, given our past account lock.
5. **Start a series: "Breaking down a real dream, Ep 1…"** One real goal broken down in Dmyor over several days (vision → milestones → today's step → progress). It gives people a reason to follow, and it shows the product without a hard sell.
6. **Show Dmyor being used.** A Passion-Planner-style hands-on reel: a phone in hand, the vision being written, the archive. This is the Dark Keynote / Living Screencast style, built once the current product flow is ready to film.
7. **Consider a face, sometimes.** Every top creator shows one. If Sanyam is willing, a 20-30 s founder clip once a week (voice + hands are fine) would test whether a human lifts reach. Otherwise hands + handwriting is the faceless compromise.
8. **Carousels, 2 a week, for saves.** "Save this" frameworks exported from our existing scenes (cheap to make). They're low reach but build a library people save.
