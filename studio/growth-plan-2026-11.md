### What the hits share
1. They correct something **everyone does every day** and believes is right (practising, sleeping in, rereading). Not a niche task (cancelling subscriptions, cleaning, recipes).
2. They reveal a **hidden mechanism** with a chart on frame 0: a curve or bar the viewer has never seen about themselves. The mechanism is the payoff, not a tip list.
3. They are about the **self** (brain, body, skill), the raw material of any goal.
4. Hook pattern: *"You [common action] because [wrong belief]. The real reason is [mechanism]."*

## 2. Topic rules (the "on-topic gate")

Every reel must pass all three before scripting:
1. **Goal link:** in one sentence, how does this help someone reach a big goal they care about? If the answer needs a stretch, drop it.
2. **Mechanism:** is there a hidden "why" we can show as a chart in frame 0? Tips without a mechanism are dropped.
3. **Universal:** would 1 in 2 adults recognise themselves in the first line?

### Four pillars (and the weekly mix for 3 reels/day = 21/week)
| Pillar | Share | What it covers | Examples that pass the gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Why goals fail (brain & behaviour)** | 8/week | motivation decay, planning fallacy, goal too big, identity, all-or-nothing, the day-4 dip, "starting Monday" | "Your motivation isn't gone. It peaked on day 1, here's the curve." |
| **B. Daily choices compound** (Dmyor's core idea) | 5/week | small choices -> trajectory; skipping vs doing; 1% maths; choice fatigue; deciding the night before | "Two people, same goal, one small daily choice: the 6-month gap." |
| **C. Learning & skill** (proven: 1,600 and 519) | 5/week | practice, memory, plateaus, feedback, learning curves | "You stopped improving at week 6. Everyone does. It's called the plateau." |
| **D. Energy for goals** (proven: sleep 1,363 / 1,297) | 3/week max | sleep, focus windows, energy dips, always tied back to doing the goal | "Your best 2 hours are wasted on email." |

Banned for now: chores, cooking, money admin (subscriptions, budgets), relationship logistics, gym programming, generic productivity hacks. They failed and they are off-brand.

## 3. Hooks

- Frame 0: the chart is already drawn and the viewer's "wrong" line is highlighted. Six words of text maximum.
- Spoken first line, one of these shapes (all from the hits):
  1. "You're not [failing at X] because [belief]. It's [mechanism]."
  2. "[Common action] is the reason [bad outcome]."
  3. "[Time] after you [start / finish X], [surprising % / amount] is gone."
  4. "The day [X] fails isn't day one. It's day [n]."
  5. "Two people. Same goal. One [tiny choice]. [Time] later:"
- Write 5 hooks per reel, keep the one a stranger would stop for; reject anything that needs context.
- Rhetorical numbers are allowed (owner 2026-10-29), but never invent a study.

## 4. Engagement and the algorithm (what actually moves Instagram Reels)

Instagram ranks Reels mostly on: watch time and completion, replays, **sends per reach** (shares in DMs), saves, then likes/comments. What we change:

1. **Length: 35-50 s.** Recent reels drifted to 65-90 s. Hits were 55-75 s, but the misses were the longest. Cap at 50 s for 2 weeks and compare (the kit's full ending takes ~14 s; compress it: one-line value close + follow line).
2. **Hold the first 3 seconds:** chart + one-line truth on frame 0, first sound in the first second (already in the kit).
3. **Mid-reel re-hook at ~15-20 s** (shorter reels, so earlier than the kit's 25 s).
4. **Loop the ending:** the last frame visually matches frame 0 (same chart, now fixed), so autoplay replays look intentional.
5. **Share trigger:** one line built to be sent ("Send this to the friend who…"), kept, but specific and in the middle, not only at the end.
6. **Save trigger:** a screenshot-worthy rule card held 2 s.
7. **Comments:** end with a question that is easy and personal ("What's the goal you keep restarting?"). Reply to every comment within the first hour (comment replies count as engagement and bring viewers back). [ASK] Comment-keyword funnel ("Comment PLAN and I'll send…"): Instagram demotes engagement bait, so only a genuine offer; needs owner OK.
8. **Captions as search:** first caption line restates the hook with keywords people search ("how to stop procrastinating", "why I can't stay consistent"). Instagram search indexes captions.
9. **Series for follows:** follows come from expecting more. Name a recurring series on screen and in the follow line, e.g. **"Why your goals fail, #12"**. Number every pillar-A reel.
10. **Posting:** strongest reel 19:30 IST (all three hits were evening or morning slots), the other two at 09:30 and 13:00. Keep 3/day, but drop any reel that fails the gate rather than fill the slot.
11. [ASK] **Trial Reels:** Instagram lets professional accounts show a reel to non-followers only before it reaches followers. Useful for A/B testing hooks on the same video (two hooks, one winner). Needs owner OK to use.
12. [ASK] **Music:** a quiet trending audio under the voice can add distribution. Explainers have no music by owner rule; propose a 1-week A/B.
13. **Pin the 3 best reels** to the top of the profile (hard-part, bad-night, wake-time) so profile visitors see proof before deciding to follow. [ASK] owner OK (the bio stays as is).
