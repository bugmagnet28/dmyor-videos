# Pixel series "Kabir vs. Kabir": scripts v1 (2026-11-07)

Same pixel world as pixel-destiny. One recurring hero (Kabir), a dry sarcastic narrator, a real mechanism shown as a picture,
a concrete fix, a loop back to frame 0, and a comment question. 45-55 s each (growth plan: 35-50 s; story reels may run a little longer).
Voice: Kokoro am_eric. No background music (owner adds music in the Instagram app). Episode badge top-left: "KABIR VS. KABIR #n".

**Series question (owner 2026-11-07, every episode):** "Why do we stop doing things we know are good for us?" Each episode answers it with one reason, shown as a card "REASON #n: ...", right after the hook.
- Ep 1, Reason #1: the motivation you planned with is gone by the time you start.
- Ep 2, Reason #2: it turns into a daily decision, and your mood makes it.
- Ep 3, Reason #3: you never notice you have stopped, because "okay" hides it.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## Ep 1: "Starting Monday"  (pillar A: why goals fail; topic: procrastination / discipline)

Frame 0: a wall calendar, every Monday circled and crossed out. Text: "HE STARTS ON MONDAY."

| Picture | Narration |
|---|---|
| Kabir in bed, Sunday night, writing "START MONDAY" on a sticky note. Calendar behind him: rows of crossed-out Mondays. | Kabir is starting on Monday. He has been starting on Monday for forty-one Mondays. |
| Card: "WHY DO WE STOP DOING THINGS WE KNOW ARE GOOD FOR US?" then "REASON #1". | So why do we stop doing things we know are good for us? Reason number one. |
| A glowing "motivation" bar above his head fills to 100% as he writes the plan. | Here's what nobody tells you. Motivation peaks the moment you make the plan. |
| Days roll by (Mon is still 3 days away). The bar drains: Fri 70%, Sat 40%, Sun 15%. | By the time Monday shows up, it's already gone. |
| Monday morning: alarm, bar at 5%, he turns over. Another Monday gets a red X. | So Monday Kabir looks at Sunday Kabir's plan... and says, absolutely not. |
| Rewind effect. Sunday night again. Bar at 100%. He does it NOW: 2 push-ups / 1 page / 5 minutes. A tiny green tick. | The fix isn't a better Monday. It's a smaller tonight. Do two minutes of it, while the bar is still full. |
| Ticks start appearing every day on the calendar, a growing green chain. | Two minutes is too small to skip. And the chain does the rest. |
| Loop: the calendar, Mondays no longer crossed out. | Kabir didn't start on Monday. He started on a Sunday night, in his pyjamas. What are you starting on Monday? Tell me, and start two minutes of it tonight. |

## Ep 2: "The gym membership"  (pillar A; topic: discipline / day-4 dip)

Frame 0: a shiny gym card on a pedestal, spotlight. Text: "HE BOUGHT DISCIPLINE."

| Picture | Narration |
|---|---|
| Kabir proudly buys a gym membership, confetti. | Kabir bought a gym membership. Twelve months. Paid in full. Discipline, purchased. |
| Card: "WHY DO WE STOP...?" then "REASON #2". | Why do we stop doing things we know are good for us? Reason number two. |
| Day 1: he runs in, glowing. Day 2: walks in. Day 3: drags in. A line chart of "excitement" drops each day. | Day one, he's unstoppable. Day two, still great. Day three... it's leg day. |
| Day 4: he stands at the door with his shoes on, then sits back on the sofa. A "?" over his head: "WHEN DO I GO TODAY?" | Day four is where it dies. Not because he's lazy. Because "when do I go today?" is now a question, and his mood answers it. |
| The card collects dust; cobwebs; a counter: "VISITS: 3 / 365". | Three visits. Very expensive card. |
| Fix: a calendar fills with fixed slots, MON 7:00, WED 7:00, FRI 7:00, like meetings. The "?" disappears. | So stop asking. Book three sessions a week, with a time, like a meeting you wouldn't skip. Missed one? Move it. Don't delete it. |
| Kabir walks in on a rainy Wednesday 7:00, not glowing, just there. | Discipline isn't a feeling. It's a decision you made on Sunday, so Wednesday doesn't have to. |
| Loop to the card, now worn and scuffed. | What's your day four? Tell me below. |

## Ep 3: "Today was okay"  (pillar B: daily choices; topic: self-reflection)

Frame 0: an open journal, seven entries in a row, all "TODAY WAS OKAY." Text: "7 DAYS OF REFLECTION."

| Picture | Narration |
|---|---|
| Kabir writes in a journal by lamplight, very serious. | Kabir started journaling. Deep self-reflection, every night. |
| The pages flip: Mon "today was okay", Tue "today was okay"... seven times. | Here's a week of it. Truly life-changing material. |
| Card: "WHY DO WE STOP...?" then "REASON #3". On the next page the gym, the book and the plan quietly fade out of the week. | Why do we stop doing things we know are good for us? Reason number three. You don't notice you've stopped. "Okay" hides it. |
| He looks into a mirror; the mirror-Kabir shrugs. | The problem isn't the journal. "How was today?" is the easiest question to lie to. |
| A new page: one question appears in gold: "WHAT DID I AVOID TODAY?" | So he changed the question. "What did I avoid today?" |
| His answers, typed: "Called no one about the job." "Skipped the hard chapter." "Didn't open the business plan." The avoided items glow. | Suddenly the journal is full. And every answer points at the thing he actually wants. |
| Next morning, he does one avoided thing first: sends the email. A tick. | You don't avoid what doesn't matter. Your avoid list is your real to-do list. |
| Loop: the journal, now one honest line per day. | So tonight, skip "how was today". Write what you avoided. What's on your list? |
