Why hitting snooze makes you more tired

Six alarms. Six snoozes. 45 extra minutes in bed, and you wake up more tired.

Every snooze starts a new nap that gets cut off 9 minutes later. That isn't rest. That's being woken up six times.

The fix:
- One alarm, set for the time you actually get up
- Put it across the room
- Open the curtain before you open your phone
