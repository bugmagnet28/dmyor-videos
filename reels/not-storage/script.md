## VO script (record this exactly)

You remember the exact moment you decided.

Where you were. What it felt like. You were so sure.

Then life did what life does. Work. Money. Tiredness you cannot explain.

And the thing you were certain about became a thing you used to talk about.

Nobody forgets their dreams. They just lose track of them.

So picture yourself ten years from now, looking back at today. Is that person hoping you
let it go?

Not a chance. They are willing you on.

So write it down for them. One line, with today's date on it.

That is what deemyor is. Phase one is live. You post what you are going to do, and it
stays there, dated, in public.

It will not do it for you. It just makes sure you never lose the receipt.

One day that archive is going to be full of people who kept their word.

Put yours in it. Today.

## What changed from the last draft

The old version opened on "Your brain is not storage." That is a **mechanism**. It is
correct and nobody cares. It explains why something happens to a person who is not in
the room yet.

This one opens by putting the viewer **inside a memory they already have**: the moment
they decided. You cannot argue with your own memory, so the first four seconds do not
have to win anything.

| old | new | why |
|---|---|---|
| "your brain is not storage" | "you remember the exact moment you decided" | a claim vs a memory |
| "nothing outside your head is holding it" | "work, money, tiredness you cannot explain" | abstraction vs the actual reason |
| "ask the version of you in ten years" | "is that person hoping you let it go? / not a chance" | a question vs an answer with heat in it |
| "it makes it checkable" | "it just makes sure you never lose the receipt" | product language vs how a person would say it |
| "an archive of what people said they were going to do" | "full of people who kept their word" | a description vs something worth being in |

**Still honest.** "It will not do it for you" is the concession, kept word for word in
spirit from your draft. Nothing here promises the dream comes true, which is the one
rule the voice guide will not bend on.

**Sendable line:** "Nobody forgets their dreams. They just lose track of them."

**The turn is "not a chance."** Everything before it is loss. Everything after is
permission. Give it the hardest visual beat in the reel.

**Followed example:** carry one dream end to end. **"run a successful business"**. It rides the card through every state, takes the
date stamp, and is the highlighted row in the archive.

**Do not recycle:** `stair.build` and `receipt.print` (both rejected), `axis.travel` /
`dream.stack` / `rows.verdict` (`letter-to-future`), `year.grid` (`days-gone`).
`holder.leak` and `phase.marks` are built and waiting.
