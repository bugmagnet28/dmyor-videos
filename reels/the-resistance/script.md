## VO script (record this exactly)

What is the difference between the people who win and the people who do not?

Both tried. Both set out to become something. One got there. One did not.

Is it willpower? Consistency? Intelligence? Or something none of us saw coming?

Look at where we are standing. More technology in your hands than any generation has ever had. More ways to change something than you could use in a lifetime.

So the answer is duller than you want it to be. You already know you could do it. You just never made a plan.

You are comfortable enough where you are that getting out of your own way feels like a bother.

That same story runs through all of history and most of the present. The dream stays a dream.

We are going to change that.

The first step has already started. There is now an intent archive, where any of us can put a dream into words the whole world can see.

So start by owning what you actually want.

Then phase two begins, shortly. Phase two is about execution: turning what you wrote into something real.

If you want to be part of this, the archive is live at www.deemyor.com. Go take your first action.
