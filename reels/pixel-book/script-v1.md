# Pixel Portal: script v1 (2026-10-26)

**Style:** pixel art animation (new engine variant of prompt-animation), 1080x1920, about 55 s.
**Voice:** first person, am_eric, calm and a little awed. Chiptune-flavoured score plus pixel sound effects.
**Main character:** a 22-year-old guitarist with a small dream. Never named, so viewers can see themselves in him.
**Theme:** the future is built by small steps you start tonight.
**Twist:** the first page of the future star's notebook is dated tonight, and in his room it is still blank.

---

## 1. HOOK (0-4 s)
- **Visual:**
  - Pixel bedroom at night. The clock reads 1:58. He lies in bed scrolling his phone, a guitar leaning against the wall.
  - The room hums. A purple pixel portal crackles open above the desk. He sits up.
  - Hard cut: he jumps straight in.
- **Text:** "I jumped 10 years into my future."
- **VO:** "Last night, a portal opened in my room. So I jumped."
- **SFX:** portal hum building, an 8-bit zap, a jump "boing", a whoosh.

## 2. THE FUTURE (4-12 s)
- **Visual:**
  - He lands in a giant arena. The year counter flashes 2036. Lights sweep, pixel confetti falls, the crowd waves phones.
  - On stage is an older him, with a beard and the same guitar. The camera pushes from young him at the back to the stage.
- **Text:** "2036"
- **VO:** "Thousands of people. Screaming one name. Mine."
- **SFX:** crowd roar (8-bit noise), guitar chord, confetti pops.

## 3. THE QUESTION / REHOOK (12-16 s)
- **Visual:**
  - Young him stares. A pixel "?" pops above his head.
  - The portal flickers behind him and the year counter starts spinning backwards: 2036, 2034, 2032...
- **Text:** "But how?"
- **VO:** "I had one question. How? So I went back. Not to the stage. To the years nobody filmed."
- **SFX:** a question "blip", rewind tape sound, counter ticks.

## 4. STRUGGLE 1: THE EMPTY BAR (16-22 s)
- **Visual:**
  - Year 2031. A dim bar with mostly empty chairs. Future him plays his heart out.
  - Two listeners: one on their phone, one is the bartender wiping a glass. He finishes, and nobody claps. He bows anyway.
- **Text:** "2031 | 2 people listening"
- **VO:** "Year five. A half-empty bar. Two people listening. One was the bartender."
- **SFX:** one lonely guitar strum, a glass squeak, silence, a single cricket chirp (comic beat).

## 5. STRUGGLE 2: THE RAIN (22-29 s)
- **Visual:**
  - Year 2029. A street corner in pixel rain. He busks with an open guitar case holding 3 coins.
  - Crowds of pixel people walk past. Nobody stops. A puddle splashes him.
  - He wipes his face and keeps playing.
- **Text:** "2029 | nobody stopped"
- **VO:** "Year three. Playing in the rain. Hundreds of people walked past. Nobody stopped. And he kept showing up anyway."
- **SFX:** rain loop, footsteps passing, a coin clink, a splash.

## 6. THE ORIGIN (29-37 s)
- **Visual:**
  - Year 2027. A tiny open mic and his very first gig. His hands shake, and he opens a worn notebook on the stool.
  - Young him looks over his shoulder. The camera zooms into the notebook's first page:
    - "Dream: play an arena."
    - "Step 1: write one song."
    - "Step 2: one open mic a month."
  - The page date types out: **Oct 26, 2026.**
- **Text:** "The first page was dated... tonight."
- **VO:** "Then I found where it started. The first page of his notebook. It was dated tonight."
- **SFX:** page flip, a typewriter tick per letter of the date, then a deep "dun" hit.

## 7. THE REALIZATION MONTAGE (37-43 s)
- **Visual:**
  - A fast pixel montage, each step checked off:
    - writing a song at the desk;
    - an open mic;
    - a bigger room;
    - a full room;
    - the arena.
  - A tally of days counts up in the corner.
- **Text:** "Not luck. Small steps."
- **VO:** "It wasn't luck. It was one small step. Then another. Every single day."
- **SFX:** a rising "right" chime per step (higher each time), plus a step counter tick.

## 8. RETURN AND ACTION (43-50 s)
- **Visual:**
  - The portal spits him back onto his bedroom floor. The clock reads 2:13.
  - He looks at the desk. The same notebook sits there, and its first page is **blank**.
  - He picks up the pen and writes the dream, then step 1. He props the guitar by the bed, turns off the lamp and gets into bed.
  - The pixel stars twinkle in the window.
- **Text:** "Step one starts tonight."
- **VO:** "The portal pulled me home. The stage was ten years away. But step one was right here. So I wrote it down. And went to sleep."
- **SFX:** portal close, landing thud, pencil scratch, lamp click, blanket rustle, soft music swell.

## 9. VALUE CLOSE AND CTA (50-55 s)
- **Visual:** Hold on the sleeping room, then fade in the end card: the Dmyor logo and @becauseidmyor. The final frame matches the opening room, so the reel loops.
- **VO:** "That's what Dmyor is for. You archive your dream today, so you can see it tomorrow, and break it down into small steps you can actually do."
- **Text:** "What would you see in 10 years? Comment below."

---

## Reel components checklist
- **Hook in 2 s:** a portal plus a time jump is a pattern interrupt, and "I jumped 10 years into my future" is a curiosity line.
- **Open loop:** "How?" holds viewers through the struggle section.
- **Rehook at about 30%:** "the years nobody filmed".
- **Emotional dip:** the empty bar and the rain show the struggle nobody saw.
- **Twist and payoff:** the notebook is dated tonight.
- **Action, not just inspiration:** he writes step one and sleeps. This follows the logic rule: the big dream breaks down to tonight's single small step.
- **Value close:** the required Dmyor line.
- **Loop:** the last frame is the first frame.
- **Comment CTA:** a question that is easy to answer.
- **Caption idea:** "The version of you in 10 years is built by what you do tonight. Write it down."
