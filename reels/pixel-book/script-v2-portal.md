# Pixel Portal: script v2 (2026-10-26)

**Message (owner):** Make your vision real.
- Create a starting point.
- Write down the big dream that looks unachievable.
- Treat the dream as a series of day-to-day decisions. You always have a choice, and your job is to make it.
- A choice doesn't have to be right the first time, as long as you reach the destination in the end.

**Style:** pixel art, 1080x1920, about 55 s, first-person voiceover (am_eric), chiptune score.
**Visual metaphor:** his life is a pixel path. Each day is a **fork** with two signs, and he has to pick one. Wrong turns are allowed; the path bends back.

---

## 1. HOOK (0-4 s)
- **Visual:** Bedroom at 1:58 AM. A portal crackles open above the desk, and he jumps in.
- **Text:** "I jumped 10 years into my future."
- **VO:** "Last night I jumped ten years into my future. To see if my dream comes true."

## 2. THE DREAM, REAL (4-9 s)
- **Visual:** 2036. An arena, lights, confetti, and the older him on stage. Young him is at the back with his mouth open.
- **Text:** "It did."
- **VO:** "It did. And from where I stand today, it looks impossible."

## 3. THE STARTING POINT (9-15 s)
- **Visual:**
  - The year counter rewinds to 2026 and the camera dives into a notebook.
  - First page: **"Dream: play an arena."** It is written shakily, with a small sticky note beside it: "lol, impossible?"
  - A pixel flag plants at the start of a long path.
- **Text:** "Step 0: write the dream that looks impossible."
- **VO:** "So I went back to find where it started. It started here. One page. A dream that looked unachievable, written down anyway. A starting point."

## 4. DAILY CHOICES (15-26 s)
- **Visual:**
  - The camera pulls out to a top-down pixel path stretching into the distance. Future him walks it.
  - At each fork, two signs pop up, and he picks one with a "right" chime:
    - "Practice | Scroll" (Practice);
    - "Write a song | Sleep in" (Write);
    - "Play the open mic | Stay home" (Play).
  - A day counter ticks: Day 1, Day 2, Day 37...
- **Text:** "A dream is a decision. Made every day."
- **VO:** "After that it was never one big leap. It was choices. Small ones. Every single day. Practice, or scroll. Show up, or stay home. There was always a choice. His only job was to make it."

## 5. WRONG TURNS (26-37 s)
- **Visual:** He takes three wrong turns, and the path bends back each time:
  - **Wrong turn 1:** the path leads to an empty bar with two listeners and no claps. A "miss" buzz plays.
  - **Wrong turn 2:** a band breaks up, and the sign cracks and falls.
  - **Wrong turn 3:** busking in the rain while everyone walks past.
  - After each one he stops, turns, and the path bends back toward the main road with an "adjust" blip.
- **Text:** "Not every choice was right."
- **VO:** "He didn't get them all right. Some turns led nowhere. An empty bar. A band that broke up. Playing in the rain while nobody stopped. But he didn't quit the path. He adjusted. And kept walking."

## 6. THE DESTINATION (37-42 s)
- **Visual:**
  - A big zoom out shows the whole path: a messy zigzag of detours that still ends at the arena.
  - A flag plants at the end with a chime chord.
- **Text:** "Not straight. But it got there."
- **VO:** "The path was never straight. But it reached the destination."

## 7. TONIGHT'S CHOICE (42-50 s)
- **Visual:**
  - The portal spits him back home. The clock reads 2:13 AM.
  - His desk becomes a fork with two signs: **"Write the dream"** or **"Keep scrolling"**. He hesitates, puts the phone face down, and writes on the blank first page.
  - Lamp off. Bed. The pixel stars twinkle.
- **Text:** "Your choice starts tonight."
- **VO:** "Then I came home. And there it was. Tonight's choice. So I made it."

## 8. VALUE CLOSE AND CTA (50-56 s)
- **Visual:** The sleeping room, then the end card with the Dmyor logo and @becauseidmyor. The last frame matches the first frame, so the reel loops.
- **VO:** "That's what Dmyor is for. You archive your dream today, so you can see it tomorrow, and break it down into small steps you can actually do."
- **Text:** "What's the choice you'll make today? Comment it."
