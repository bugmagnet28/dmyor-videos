## VO script (record this exactly)

There are four months left in 2026.

Think back to January. How many of the things you meant to do have you actually done?

For most people the honest answer is: not many.

Here is the part worth understanding. It did not fall apart in one big moment. You skipped one day, and nothing happened. So skipping felt free. You skipped the next one, and still nothing happened.

That is the trap. Nothing was counting. When nothing is counting, missing a day costs you nothing on the day you miss it. It only costs you eight months later, all at once.

Nobody fails in a day. You fail in the days nobody counted.

So make the days visible. Write the thing down, then log every day you move on it. Once you can see the record building, you stop wanting to break it. That is a habit, and a habit does not run on willpower.

That is what deemyor does. Phase one is live today: write your dream down, dated, public, permanent.

Phase two starts shortly. That is the part that helps you actually get there.

The archive is live at www.deemyor.com
