# four months forward

target: ~90s at 2.61 w/s
engine: motion (colour, kinetic type, camera)

## VO script (record this exactly)

Four months from today, it is January.

What is different, if you actually do the thing you keep telling yourself you will do?

Not the big version of it. The small one. Twenty minutes a day. One page. One phone call.

Four months is about a hundred and twenty days. A hundred and twenty days of almost anything changes something.

The work is not the hard part. The hard part is that in three weeks you will not remember you said this.

So here is the whole method. It takes five minutes to set up.

Write one sentence. Not a feeling. A sentence someone else could check. Not get fit. Walk thirty minutes.

Then pick the smallest version that still counts on a bad day. If your normal is an hour, your bad day is ten minutes. Ten minutes still counts.

Then mark it. Every single day. On paper, on a wall, anywhere you will see it without going to look for it.

You will miss days. Everyone misses days. The only rule that matters is this. Never miss twice.

At thirty days it stops feeling like a decision. At sixty you stop noticing you are doing it. At a hundred and twenty, you are just someone who does this.

That is the whole thing. Write the sentence tonight.

In January you will either have it, or you will have the same sentence you have now.
