## Instagram caption
Why highlighting doesn't help you remember

Twenty-four pages, all yellow. Exam day, all you remember is the colour.

Rereading your highlights feels like knowing it, because the page looks familiar. But the exam never shows you the page. It makes you pull the answer out of your head.

The fix:
- Turn each heading into a question
- Cover the page and answer it out loud
- What you can't answer is what you study next
