## VO script (record this exactly)

If you could send one message to yourself in five years, what would it say?

Not advice. Just the list. The things you actually want.

Because right now you are full of plans you have not started yet.

And that does not last on its own. It fades quietly. You do not notice the day it goes.

So write them down. Dated. Not a diary nobody opens, a list with a date on it.

Then in 2031 you get to check. Did I do that one. Did I get close.

Some you will have forgotten you ever wanted. One or two you will have already passed
without noticing.

That is the fun part. The checking.

So what would you write to your future self?

Put it in the comments.

**Sendable line:** "It fades quietly. You do not notice the day it goes."

**Followed example:** carry ONE dream end to end so the payoff lands on something the
viewer has already met. Use **"buy my parents a house"**, the example the voice guide
itself holds up. It rides the axis card, takes the date stamp, and is the row that
resolves `done` on "did I do that one?".

**The dreams are the ones people grind for**, not small tidy ones: a house for your
parents, your own company, another country, a novel, a sub three hour marathon. Modest
goals make the payoff feel small and the reel forgettable.

**Do not recycle:** `year.grid` was `days-gone` and `daygrid.fill` is worn out. This reel
needs its own scenes: a time axis carrying a record forward, and rows that resolve into
verdicts.
