## What the data says
1. **The topic gate + series card did not lift views; if anything they dropped.** Only one V-EXP-34 reel passed 300 (first-ten-minutes, 457). Older reels are 4-6 days in, so age is not the whole story.
2. **Every hit is a concrete physical scene with a number on frame 0** that corrects something people do: "8 weeks. Still 40 kg." (1,357), "After a bad night, don't sleep in" (1,364), weekend wake-time line (1,309), practising the easy part (1,601). The V-EXP-34 reels are about psychology in the abstract (out of sight, the daily decision, just today) and all land ~110-170.
3. **The series card ("Why do we stop doing things we know are good for us? Reason #n") repeats in every reel at ~3-8 s.** Same words every time is a strong swipe signal for returning viewers and delays the payoff. Hypothesis, needs a test.
4. **Pixel Kabir reels perform like explainers on Instagram (112, 117)** but are the best on YouTube (kabir-1: 49 views vs 8-24 for the explainers posted with it).
5. **Length:** the 61 s same-weight beat every <=50 s reel. Shorter alone is not the lever.
6. **Cadence broke:** nothing posted Mon 9 / Tue 10, then 4 reels in 2 days. Consistency of daily slots matters for distribution.
7. **YouTube is near zero** (0-49 views per short). X not measured this run.
