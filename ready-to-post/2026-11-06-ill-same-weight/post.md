# ill-same-weight (animated illustration explainer, 61.22s)

## Caption
If you've lifted the same weight for two months, you're not training. You're maintaining.

24 sessions at 40 kg never asked your body for more than it could already do. The fix is double progression, and all it needs is a notebook:
1. Pick a rep range, 8 to 10
2. Every session, try to add one rep (8 8 8, then 9 9 8, then 10 10 9)
3. When all three sets hit 10, add 2.5 kg and drop back to 8

Same gym, same days. Write every set down.
