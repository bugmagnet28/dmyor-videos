## Caption
How to set a realistic deadline for your goal.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #23: the guessed deadline. Arjun gives himself 4 weeks for his portfolio. Week 4, it's half done, he feels behind and stops opening it. The goal was fine. The date was a guess from the best case.

In one study (Buehler, Griffin & Ross, 1994), students guessed their thesis would take about 34 days. It took about 56.

The fix: before you set a date, ask how long the last similar thing actually took you, and plan that.
