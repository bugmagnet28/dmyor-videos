## Spoken script
You don't need a perfect resume. You need one clear sentence. 

 When someone asks what you do, most people say a job title, and then they ramble. Marketing, I guess, a bit of everything, it kind of depends. The other person nods, and forgets it by lunch. 

 So write one sentence with three parts. Who you help, what you help them do, and your proof. 

 For example. I help local cafés fill their quiet weekday hours, proven by a loyalty card that brought one café thirty new regulars. 

 Or for a developer. I help small clinics cut missed appointments with text reminders, proven by the system I built for my dentist. 

 Then put it everywhere. Your profile headline, the top of your resume, and the first line of every message you send. 

 Here's the test. Read it to a friend, and ask them to say it back. If they can't repeat it, cut words until they can. 

 Write yours right now, in under twenty five words. Who you help, what you do, and your proof. 

 That's what Dmyor is for. You archive your dream today, so you can see it tomorrow, and break it down into small steps you can actually do. 

 If you want to try it out, I've put the link in the bio.
