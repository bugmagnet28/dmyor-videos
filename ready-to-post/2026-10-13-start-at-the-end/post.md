# start-at-the-end (14.6s, no VO)

## Caption
Big goal, no idea where to start? Start at the end.

Goal: run a 10k in 3 months.
Step back: month 2, run 5k nonstop.
Step back: week 1, three short runs.
Step back: today, shoes by the door.

Keep stepping back until the step fits in today. Then do that one.

Save this and try it on your goal. What's yours? 👇
