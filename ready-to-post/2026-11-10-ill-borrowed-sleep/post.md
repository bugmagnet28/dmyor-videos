# ill-borrowed-sleep (animated illustration explainer, 50.0s)

## Caption
Why staying up late to work on your goal backfires.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #19: borrowed sleep. Dev codes his app from 11 PM to 1 AM. Week one feels great. By week three the same two hours finish almost nothing, and he decides he's not good at this.

The hidden part: sleep researchers found that on six hours a night, people got worse at attention tests nearly every day, but said they felt only a little sleepy. You can't feel the drop.

The fix:
- Lights out at a fixed time
- Goal time before work, not after midnight
- Feeling fine isn't proof you're rested
