## Caption
Your 4 PM coffee is still in you at bedtime.

Caffeine takes about 5 hours to drop by half. A cup at 4: half of it at 9 PM, a quarter at 2 AM. You can still fall asleep, but caffeine can keep that sleep lighter. Then you wake up tired and reach for another coffee.

The cut-off rule:
1. Bedtime minus 8 hours (bed at 11 = cut-off 3 PM)
2. Decaf after that
3. 4 PM slump? A 10-minute walk outside

Swap that one cup: at bedtime you go from about half a cup to about an eighth.
