## Caption
Why you stop improving at a skill after the first month (and how to fix the plateau).

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #25: autopilot. Month one, you go from nothing to five songs. Then months of the same five songs, and your level doesn't move. Once practice feels easy, your brain runs it on autopilot. Easy practice keeps a skill. It doesn't grow it. It's why you've typed every day for years and you're no faster.

The fix, every session:
- Notice what feels easy
- Add one thing just out of reach (the song 5 beats faster, one page that's a bit too hard)
- Fix what breaks
