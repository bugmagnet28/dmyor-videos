## Caption
Want to switch careers? Don't start with a 6-month course. Start with 3 conversations.

• Find 3 people who already do the job (LinkedIn, alumni, a friend of a friend)
• Send one short message
• Ask: What's a normal week like? What do you wish you knew earlier? What should I learn first?

Three conversations this week beat three months of guessing.
