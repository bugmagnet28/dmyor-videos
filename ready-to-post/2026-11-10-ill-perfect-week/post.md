## Caption
How to stay consistent with a workout plan: plan for your real week, not a perfect one.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #18: the perfect week. We plan seven runs, real life gives us four, and four out of seven reads as a fail. A few weeks of "failing" and we quit, even though four runs a week is good.

The fix:
- Count what you actually did in the last two weeks
- That number is your plan
- Hit it two weeks in a row? Add one

Same four runs, now a hundred percent, and that's a week you want to repeat.
