## Spoken script
You're not getting better, because you keep practising the part you can already do. 

 Learning guitar? Most people play the song from the top every time. The verse sounds great, they mess up the same four bars in the chorus, and they start over. So the easy part gets fifty plays, and the hard part gets five. 

 Flip it. Find the exact spot where you break down, and practise only that. 

 Loop those four bars, slow enough to get them right. Ten clean times, then nudge the speed up a little. After ten minutes, play the whole song once. 

 Same with a language. If speaking is the hard part, another vocabulary lesson won't fix it. Record yourself talking about your day for one minute, every day. 

 Here's the test. If practice feels easy, you're rehearsing, not improving. 

 Next time you practise, start with your weakest part, for ten minutes, before anything else. 

 That's what Dmyor is for. You archive your dream today, so you can see it tomorrow, and break it down into small steps you can actually do.
