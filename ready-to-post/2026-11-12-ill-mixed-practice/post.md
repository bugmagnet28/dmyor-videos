## Caption
How to study for exams so it sticks: mix your practice.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #21: the practice that feels best teaches you least. Riya does twenty problems of one type, then twenty of the next. Almost all right. Then the exam mixes them up and she blanks: she knew how to solve them, not which method to pick.

In one study (Rohrer & Taylor, 2007), students who mixed their practice problems scored about three times higher a week later.

The fix:
- One of each type, in a new order every time
- Expect it to feel slower
- Getting more wrong in practice is the learning
