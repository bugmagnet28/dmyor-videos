## Instagram caption
How to get faster at anything: practise slower

Practising fast doesn't make you fast. It makes you fast at your mistakes. Every rep teaches your hands something; sloppy reps teach sloppy.

Slow down until it's clean. Ten clean reps. Then speed up a little. Still clean? Speed up again. Typing, guitar, a tennis serve: same rule.

What are you rushing right now? 👇
