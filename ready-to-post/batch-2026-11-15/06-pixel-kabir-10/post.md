## Instagram caption
How to practise a presentation (out loud, not in your head)

Kabir read his slides 20 times. Silently. Flawless. Next morning: he opens his mouth and nothing comes out. His brain knew the words; his mouth had never met them.

Reading in your head and saying it out loud are different skills. Rehearse out loud, standing, 3 times, once to a friend. The first run sounds terrible. That's the run that counts.
