## Instagram caption
The study method that feels worse but works better

Two students, same chapter, same 3 hours. Sam reads it 3 times: it feels easy, familiar. Dev reads it once, closes the book and writes down everything he remembers. It feels awful. He gets half wrong.

One week later, Dev remembers more. Pulling it back out is what makes it stay. Familiar isn't the same as remembered.

Are you a Sam or a Dev? 👇
