## Caption
Why you can't stay consistent: you're deciding every day.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #15: the daily decision. Win the argument with yourself 9 days out of 10 and the chance you haven't skipped is about half after a week, and 4% after a month (0.9 to the power of 30).

The fix: decide once, in one sentence. After [something I already do], I do [the step].
- After lunch, I walk 15 minutes
- After I brush my teeth, I read 10 pages
- After coffee, 10 minutes of guitar
