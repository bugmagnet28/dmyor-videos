You don't need a free week for a big goal. You need three half-hours. 

 Even a packed week has them. Before work, a lunch break, Sunday morning. But finding them is the easy part. 

 Say the goal is your first podcast episode. Tonight, put three thirty-minute blocks in your calendar. 

 Then give each block one output, not a topic. Monday, outline episode one. Wednesday, record a ten-minute test. Sunday, listen back and fix one thing.
