## Caption
How to stay motivated halfway through a long goal: the middle problem.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #27: the middle. Month one of learning Spanish, every word feels like a win. Month six, today's ten words feel like nothing. At the start you measure from zero, near the end the finish pulls you. In the middle you're far from both.

The fix:
- A finish line every month (month one: order dinner in Spanish)
- First half: count what's done
- Second half: count what's left
