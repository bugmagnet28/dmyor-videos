## Caption
Your room doesn't get messy on Sunday. It gets messy ten minutes at a time.

Meera spent 4 hours every Sunday cleaning, and by Wednesday the flat looked the same again. So she switched to a 10-minute reset:
1. Every night at 9 PM, a 10-minute timer
2. One rule: everything goes back where it lives
3. Only three things: dishes in the sink, clothes off the chair, desk cleared

Two weeks later her Sunday was free, and the week took 70 minutes instead of 240.
