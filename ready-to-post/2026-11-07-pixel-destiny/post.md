## Instagram caption
Can you write your own destiny? Or are you just hoping to get lucky?

3 steps:
1. Possibility: is it possible? The less likely you think it is, the harder it gets. Harder, not impossible.
2. Path: there's never one way. Pick the one that's best for you right now.
3. Execution: break the goal into tasks, then into one stair you can take today.

Then repeat steps 2 and 3, again and again.

What's the one stair you'll take today?
