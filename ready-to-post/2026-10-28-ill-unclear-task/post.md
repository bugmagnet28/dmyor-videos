## Spoken script
- [hook] You don't procrastinate on hard tasks. You procrastinate on unclear ones.
- Look at a normal to-do list. Taxes. Website. Thesis. None of those is something you can actually start. Your brain can't see the first move, so it picks up the phone instead.
- [turn] The fix is to rewrite each item as the next visible action. Start with a verb, and make it something you could see is done.
- So taxes becomes, download the March bank statement. Website becomes, write the headline for the home page. And thesis becomes, write two hundred words of section two point one.
- Here's the test. If you can't picture the moment it's done, it's still too vague. Split it again until you can.
- [land] Take the task you've been avoiding the longest, and rewrite it right now. Then do just that one line.
- [outro] That's what Dmyor is for. You archive your dream today, so you can see it tomorrow, and break it down into small steps you can actually do.
