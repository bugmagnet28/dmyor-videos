# plan-the-bad-day (14.2s, no VO)

## Caption
Your plan only works on good days. So give it a bad-day version.

Good day: run 5k. Bad day: run 1 lap.
Good day: write a page. Bad day: write 1 line.
Good day: study 2 hours. Bad day: open the book.

The bad-day version is tiny on purpose. It keeps the chain going when the day goes wrong.

What's your bad-day version? Send this to the friend who quits on day 3.
