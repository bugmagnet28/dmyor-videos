## Caption
If it's dark when you wake up, your body thinks it's still night.

A viewer asked: class at 7:30, up at 6:45, no sunlight. Your body clock sets itself by light, and a room lamp is dim next to even a grey sky. If your first daylight is lunch, the morning signal comes hours late.

The dark morning rule:
1. Every light on the moment you're up, breakfast next to the brightest one
2. Outside at the first daylight you get, before 10 if you can (a grey sky counts)
3. Same wake time every day, give or take an hour on weekends
