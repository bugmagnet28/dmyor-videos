## Spoken script
You don't need a budget with twenty categories. You need a limit on one. 

 Most budgets die in week two. Tracking every coffee and every bus ticket turns into a second job, so you stop opening the spreadsheet, and the money leaks out anyway. 

 So do this instead. Open your bank app, scroll through last month, and add up the one category that leaks the most. For a lot of people, that's food delivery. 

 Say it came to two hundred and forty dollars. Set a cap of forty a week. Four weeks of forty is one sixty, so that's eighty dollars you keep, every month. 

 Then every Sunday, check one number. Under forty? That week is a win. Over? Don't quit. Next week starts again at zero. 

 It works for any leak. Clothes, games, coffee on the way to work. Just pick one, not five. 

 Tonight, open your bank app, find your biggest leak, and write down your weekly cap.
