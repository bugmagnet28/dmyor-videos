## Spoken script
Five goals at once usually means zero progress. 

 Think about it. With work and everything else, you've got maybe five or six free hours a week. 

 Split that five ways, and each goal gets about an hour. That's not enough to move any of them. 

 So pick one main goal for the next twelve weeks. Just one. 

 Say it's launching a side business. That goal gets your five best hours every week. 

 Not whatever leftover time you have at the end of the day. 

 The other goals don't disappear. They go on the minimum, just enough to keep them alive. 

 So if you're a runner, that's two easy runs a week. 

 You keep your fitness, you're just not training for a race right now. 

 And anything you can't even do the minimum for, park it on a list with a date, like January first, when you'll look at it again. 

 Because one goal that's actually moving beats five goals that are all stuck. 

 That's what Dmyor is for. You archive your dream today, so you can see it tomorrow, 

 and break it down into small steps you can actually do. 

 If you want to try it out, I've put the link in the bio.
