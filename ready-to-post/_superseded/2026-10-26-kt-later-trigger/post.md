## Spoken script
Later isn't a time. 

 When you say you'll do it later, there's nothing in your day that actually says later. 

 So it just never comes. 

 So give the habit a trigger instead, something you already do every single day. 

 And use this sentence. After I do this, I will do that. 

 Like, after I pour my morning coffee, I'll go through ten Spanish flashcards. 

 After I close my laptop for the day, I'll go for a twenty minute walk. 

 After I finish dinner, I'll read ten pages of my book. 

 So pick the one habit you keep putting off, and attach it to something you already do. 

 Then write that sentence down tonight. 

 That's what Dmyor is for. You archive your dream today, so you can see it tomorrow, 

 and break it down into small steps you can actually do. 

 If you want to try it out, I've put the link in the bio.
