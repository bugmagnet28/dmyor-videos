## Caption
Why you can't stay consistent: "just today" adds up.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #20: just today. Every skip feels like one day. Tired on Tuesday, busy on Thursday. But you never make that choice once. Twice a week is 104 days a year: three months of your goal.

Behaviour researchers call it choice bracketing: judge the pattern, not the day.

The fix:
- Before you skip, ask: would I be okay skipping this every week?
- If not, do the 10-minute version today
- 10 minutes still counts as a day
