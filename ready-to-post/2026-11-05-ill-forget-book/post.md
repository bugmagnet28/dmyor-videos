## Caption
A week after you finish a book, most of it is gone.

Not because your memory is bad. 300 pages, about 6 hours of reading, and a week later you can explain it in one sentence. Memory drops fast in the first day, then keeps sliding, and rereading or highlighting barely slows it down. Pulling it back out does.

The 3-line rule:
1. When you close the book, write 3 lines from memory: what it said, one example, one thing you'll do
2. Next day, cover them and say them again
3. A week later, once more

Under 3 minutes in total.
