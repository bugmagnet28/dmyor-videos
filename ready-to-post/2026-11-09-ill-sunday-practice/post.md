## Caption
How to learn a skill faster: stop practising only on Sundays.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #17: the big session. Two hours once a week feels serious, but by next Sunday most of it has faded, and you spend the start catching up. And one busy Sunday makes the whole week a zero.

Your brain locks in a new skill between sessions, a lot of it while you sleep. So split the same time:
- Piano: 20 min after dinner
- Code: 20 min before work
- Spanish: 20 min on your commute

Same 140 minutes, 7 sessions, and a missed day costs 20 minutes, not a week.
