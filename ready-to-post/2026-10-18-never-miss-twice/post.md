## Caption
Missed a day? That's not failure. That's a normal week.

The streak breaks on day TWO. One miss is an accident. Two in a row is the start of a new habit: not doing it.

So the only rule: never miss twice. Bad day? Do the smallest version (1 page, 1 push-up, 5 minutes). It still counts.

Save this for the next time you miss a day.

("Never miss twice" is James Clear's rule from Atomic Habits.)
