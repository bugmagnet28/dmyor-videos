## Spoken script
You can know four hundred words and still freeze ordering a coffee. 

 That's what happened to Ana. And the fix wasn't more words. It was one list she wrote in ten minutes. 

 Ana was moving to Madrid in twelve weeks. So every night, she learned twenty new words in an app. 

 Six weeks in, she knew four hundred words. Then she walked into a café, and couldn't say one full sentence. The words weren't wrong. She'd just never said them out loud, together. 

 So what did she change? 

 She wrote down the twenty sentences she actually says in a normal day. A coffee, please. Can you say that slower? Where's the metro? 

 She translated them once. Then every morning, she said all twenty out loud, and recorded one minute. About five minutes a day. 

 Week ten, Ana ordered, asked for the bill, and chatted with her landlord. In Spanish. 

 Here's the rule. Write the twenty sentences you say every day. Say them out loud each morning. Record one minute. Send this to the friend with a long streak who still can't order a coffee.
