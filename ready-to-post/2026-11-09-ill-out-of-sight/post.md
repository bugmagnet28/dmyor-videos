## Caption
Why you lose motivation for your goals by week three: you stopped seeing them.

Why we stop doing things we know are good for us, reason #16: out of sight. Week one you think about the goal every day. By week three it only comes up when someone asks, because nothing in your day points at it. It lives in a notes app you never open.

The fix:
- Put the goal and this week's one step on your lock screen
- You see it every time you pick up your phone
- Every Sunday, 2 minutes: cross off this week's step, write the next one
